The Crisis

The Triple Exclusion Trapping
2.2 Million Kenyans

Africa's largest untapped workforce is locked out of the continent's fastest-growing industry.

Physical Barriers

In Nairobi's informal settlements, a person with contractures spends 3+ hours commuting to jobs non-disabled residents reach in 45 minutes. Unpaved paths, inaccessible transport, and workplaces without ramps make traditional employment economically unviable.

3× longer commutes

Skills-Opportunity Mismatch

Kenya's "Silicon Savannah" — 200+ companies valued at $1+ billion — faces 20,000+ unfilled tech roles. Yet under 1% of persons with disabilities access technical training. Existing programs are physically inaccessible and cost KES 150,000–300,000.

<1% PWDs in tech training

Systemic Discrimination

82% of Kenyan employers won't interview qualified PWD candidates, citing "productivity concerns." Digital job platforms lack accessibility — AI screening algorithms filter out employment gaps common in PWD resumes.

82% employer rejection rate
Our Solution

Four Pillars of
Inclusive Employment

A comprehensive approach that transforms both the supply side (PWD skills) and demand side (employer practices) simultaneously.

1

Accessible Training Platform

AI-powered e-learning built WCAG 2.1 AA compliant from ground up — not retrofitted. Three tracks: Software Development (Python, HTML/CSS/JS), Digital Freelancing (Upwork/Fiverr), and Specialized Tech (data analysis, digital marketing).

70% async + 30% in-person labs
2

AI Job-Matching Engine

Machine learning algorithm trained on 500+ remote tech jobs, matching users by disability type, accommodation needs, skills, and preferences. Bias mitigation removes disability discrimination from recommendations.

World's first disability-aware job AI
3

3D-Printed Adaptive Devices

Custom ergonomic peripherals — one-handed keyboards, foot-operated mice, trackball alternatives — at KES 2,000 ($15) vs. commercial KES 15,000 ($115). Designed from lived experience.

85% cost savings
4

Employer Partnerships

"Try Before You Buy" subsidized internships (50% salary co-funded) converting to full employment. Corporate disability inclusion training and quota compliance support for Kenya's 5% PWD hiring mandate.

Solving both sides of the equation

Meet the Founder

Paul Lawrence

"The 12× income gap between me and my disabled neighbors is unacceptable. I'm going to close it."

I don't just build for persons with disabilities — I AM a person with disability. My contractures affect my joints and mobility daily. Every feature in AccessTech Kenya comes from lived experience: the keyboard shortcuts that reduce wrist strain exist because I NEED them.

When 12 persons with disabilities couldn't access my first digital literacy program's 2nd-floor venue — that failure planted AccessTech's seed. I converted personal adversity into systemic solutions.
